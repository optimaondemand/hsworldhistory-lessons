📚 WHAT THIS MODULE CONTAINS

This Teacher's Module holds the two things optima-world-history Part II requires: (1) the course syllabus (both the Live and On-Demand variants, each with a downloadable, teacher-editable .docx and an in-Canvas preview) and (2) this manual, which indexes every satisfied CPALMS benchmark, week by week, plus the comprehensive alignment matrix. This module is visible to teachers only and is never published to students.

⚠️ FLAGGED CONTENT DECISIONS

Week 5, Primary Source 2 substitution. The Course Map ODT names “Charter of Modern Thought” as Week 5's second primary source, which is not a real historical document title. The build uses the Admonitio Generalis (789) instead, a genuine and scholarly-defensible Carolingian capitulary. This substitution was a deliberate course-lead decision (2026-08-01), not a silent swap, and is flagged here per optima-hs-social-studies IV.1's no-silent-substitution rule.

Primary sources are typeset, not scanned. All 36 primary sources across the course are typeset HTML rather than photocopies or scans of an original printed source, several already quoting real public-domain translations with citations (e.g. Augustine via Marcus Dods, 1871). A re-sourcing pass to locate scans or verifiable public-domain quotations for all 36 was planned, then cancelled by the course lead on 2026-08-02 as low-yield relative to its cost. This is the course's final, accepted state, not a gap awaiting a future pass.

📋 STANDARDS PHILOSOPHY, FOR REFERENCE

Per CLAUDE.md: every benchmark must be satisfied by at least one lesson students read and at least one assessment students submit; every assessment must satisfy at least one benchmark; benchmarks are distributed reasonably across lessons rather than built to a one-to-one checklist; and benchmark codes never render on a student-facing page (they were stripped from all 124 offending files at Stage B1 and now live here and in the two standards pages instead).
