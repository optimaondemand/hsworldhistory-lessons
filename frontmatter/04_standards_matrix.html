⚠️ APPENDIX A: STANDARDS GAP (W.2.6, ICONOCLASM HALF)

SS.912.W.2.6 (Iconoclast Controversy half only) has no natural home in the assigned weekly primary sources: Week 2's sources are Justinian-era, roughly two centuries too early for the 8th–9th century Iconoclast Controversy. Appendix A supplies a primary source exercise on the controversy (excerpt from the Acts of the Second Council of Nicaea, 787), cross-referenced as supplementary reading for the Week 2 Byzantine unit or as background before Week 9's treatment of the 1054 Schism. The Schism half of W.2.6 is assessed at Week 9; the code as a whole is therefore satisfied, even though no single item tags both halves.

⚠️ EIGHT DOCUMENTED GAPS: NONE BLOCKING

The GAP rows above (nine codes; eight distinct findings, since the four ELA.EE codes count as one finding) are real and verified, not estimated, but all are small and fixable without writing new content: each is one lesson-paragraph's worth of material that a VR activity was presumably meant to assess but the VR item carries no benchmark strip to confirm it. Recommended fix, per standards-index.md's own "Gaps found" section: add a benchmark strip to the relevant VR file, or add one question to an existing quiz/CQ, the next time that file is touched. None of the eight require a new lesson, a new assessment, or a course-map change.

Full reasoning behind each gap, including why W.2.1 (never tagged anywhere, not just VR-shaped) is the one closest to a genuine content gap rather than a tagging omission, is in standards-index.md (course root).
